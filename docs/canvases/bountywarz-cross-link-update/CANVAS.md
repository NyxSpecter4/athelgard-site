## 🎯 Purpose

**Current Problem:** BountyWarz presents Athelgard strongly, but doesn't explain that she also exists **outside the game** as a builder and voice guide.

**Solution:** Add strategic cross-links that make the connection to Athelgard.io explicit, without disrupting the current strong gameplay narrative.
